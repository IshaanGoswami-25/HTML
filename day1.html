About the Institute

Pranveer Singh Institute of Technology (PSIT) in Kanpur is a premier private institute established in 2004, offering UG, PG, and diploma programs in Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, and Computer Applications. Known for its NAAC 'A++++++++++' accreditation and high-profile placements, it is a leading hub for professional education in Uttar Pradesh.

Key Highlights Flagship Programs: B.Tech, MBA, MCA, BBA, BCA, and B.Pharm. Campus Life: Spread over a lush 140-acre campus on NH-19 (Bhauti, Kanpur), it is widely praised on forums like Quora for its excellent amenities, including smart AC classrooms, a centralized library, and well-maintained hostels. Placements: The institute maintains strong industry ties with mass recruiters and global tech companies like TCS, Amazon, Deloitte, and Wipro. Fees & Scholarships: Base tuition fees generally start around Rs. 1,09,296 per annum, with scholarships reaching up to 100% for high-ranking JEE Main students.
